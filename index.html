OUR STORY, OUR EXPERTISE

Lorem Ipsum Tattoo was founded in 9621 B.C. by a family of talented tattooists. We have been proudly serving the Ocean City with incredible art ever since.

Our studio now boasts five experienced tattoo artists specialising in a wide range of styles, from traditional and neo-traditional to colour realism, fine line, and everything in between. We recently welcomed a professional body piercer to the team, who is also completing her tattooing apprenticeship with us. Check out our artists page to learn more about our staff and their expertise.
